Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · ScanInbox and ScanInbox Google Ads

ScanInbox is a planned inbox.eu service. This website currently explains the idea and collects expressions of interest. It does not currently accept payments, upload scans or provide document delivery. This policy covers the website and waitlist, and the internal Google Ads tool used by our authorized team to plan and measure advertising.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

The data controller is SIA INBOKSS, registration number 40003560720, Matrožu iela 15, 2nd floor, Rīga, LV-1048, Latvia. For privacy questions, access requests, correction, withdrawal of consent or deletion, email support@inbox.lv and mention ScanInbox. You can also reply to a ScanInbox message to withdraw your sign-up consent. We delete your waitlist entry and linked answers within three working days of a deletion request.

2. Waitlist information

When you sign up, we collect your email address, consent, selected page language and submission timestamps. You may optionally answer questions about your use case, device count and printer brands. These answers are linked to your sign-up. Submission records help us process updates and prevent abuse. Our waitlist database does not store IP addresses or browser user agents.

We use this information to contact you about ScanInbox and understand demand. Signing up and answering follow-up questions are voluntary; without an email address and consent, we cannot add you to the waitlist. The legal basis for waitlist contact and the associated survey is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). We do not use your address for unrelated advertising or newsletters, or sell it.

We use waitlist information until the service launches and for no longer than 24 months from sign-up. If the service has not launched within that period, sign-ups are deleted automatically. You can request earlier deletion at any time. Any later service registration will have its own information and choices. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Deletion requests also cover the linked survey and submission records.

3. Website, cookies and measurement

Netlify hosts this website, and Supabase processes waitlist submissions in its EU region (Ireland). Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses, device/browser information and security logs to deliver and protect their services, even though those fields are absent from our waitlist database. Our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the website are the basis for necessary technical processing (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

The site remembers language selection using the scaninbox_lang cookie (up to one year) and theme selection using browser local storage. Cookiebot manages cookie choices. Google Tag Manager loads measurement tags, including Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads. These services can process page visits, interactions, device/browser information, online identifiers, referral information and conversion events. A successful new sign-up generates a lead event; the site's lead event contains no email address or survey answers.

Analytics and advertising storage default to denied. Where consent is required, optional cookies and storage depend on your choice in the cookie banner. Google Consent Mode may send limited, cookieless measurement signals when storage is denied; denying cookies does not mean that every technical request to Google stops. Google Fonts also receives technical request information when the browser loads the site's fonts.

You can revisit cookie choices using the cookie controls on the site, clear cookies or local storage in your browser, and use browser controls to restrict storage. Optional analytics and advertising cookies are based on consent. You can sign up without consenting to optional analytics or advertising cookies.

4. Internal Google Ads tool and Google user data

ScanInbox Google Ads is an internal tool for authorized team members. Visitors do not need to sign in with Google or grant access to their Google accounts to join the waitlist.

When a team member authorizes Google Ads access, the tool accesses the permitted advertising account information needed for campaign planning, keyword research and reporting: account and campaign settings, keywords, advertising costs, performance and conversion statistics. OAuth authorization credentials are held locally with access restricted to the authorized operator; they are not included in public pages, repositories or reports. The tool does not request access to Gmail, personal email contents, Google Drive or contacts.

Google Ads account data is used only to manage, plan and evaluate ScanInbox advertising. Access is limited to the authorized team and infrastructure needed for that purpose. We do not sell Google user data, use it for unrelated purposes, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Account access and local credentials can be revoked when no longer needed; team members can also revoke authorization through their Google Account permissions. Local working data and reports are retained only while needed for the advertising experiment and its evaluation, subject to applicable accounting or legal recordkeeping obligations.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Authorized inbox.eu team members and service providers receive information needed for their roles. Providers include Netlify for hosting, Supabase for waitlist storage, Cookiebot for consent management, and Google for fonts and measurement. We do not send waitlist email addresses to Google in the lead event. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

The waitlist database is hosted in the EU. Some providers may process technical or measurement information outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers must use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as European Commission standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

Provider information: Netlify privacy, Supabase privacy, Cookiebot privacy and Google privacy. Cookie and measurement retention varies by the provider, cookie and account configuration; the cookie banner provides the available cookie details. These records are separate from our waitlist retention period.

6. Your rights and security

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above. You can complain to Latvia's Data State Inspectorate or your local supervisory authority.

We use HTTPS and restrict access to stored sign-ups. The public submission endpoint can submit or update a sign-up but cannot list the waitlist. We do not use these sign-ups to make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

7. Changes and future service

We will update this page if processing changes. The date above identifies the latest version. Before offering document scanning or delivery, we will provide information covering that service; this waitlist policy does not describe processing of future scanned documents.
